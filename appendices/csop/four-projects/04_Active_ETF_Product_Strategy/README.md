# Point 4 - Active ETF Product Strategy

## Core Story

This point demonstrates how regulatory and market research can support a new-product decision: strategy suitability, transparency, scalability, investor fit, distribution, and operational readiness.

## File Map

- `内地主动ETF.pptx` - Core strategy deck. Covers Mainland active-ETF rules, differences versus passive/index-enhancement products, U.S. market evolution, product mix, fund flows, leading products, and adviser-heavy ownership.
- `产品进度.docx` - Supporting team context showing the cross-functional launch checklist across legal, Capital Markets, market makers, seed investors, product, operations, trading, IT, and distribution. Treat this as background context unless you directly worked on the checklist.

## What This Proves

- Ability to move beyond descriptive cross-market comparison and draw product-design implications.
- Understanding that active ETF success depends on more than investment performance: disclosure, capacity, liquidity, fees, target investors, and channel fit also matter.
- Ability to frame research as decision questions for product, investment, Capital Markets, and distribution teams.

## Interview Guardrail

Position this as strategy support, not as ownership of a product launch. The strongest value add is the decision framework you derived from regulation, U.S. precedents, and investor structure.
