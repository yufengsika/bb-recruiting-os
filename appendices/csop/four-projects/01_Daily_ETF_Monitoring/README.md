# Point 1 - Daily ETF Monitoring

## Core Story

This point demonstrates recurring ETF Capital Markets coverage: primary-market creations/redemptions, secondary-market liquidity, Southbound demand, QDII constraints, and premium-control measures.

## File Map

- `每日检测-汇总版 0630.xlsx` - Main daily monitoring workbook. Covers cross-border ETFs, A500, Southbound-eligible products, gold, MPF, fund flows, AUM, shares, turnover, and creation/redemption limits.
- `Southbound holdings-CM  - Copy.xlsx` - Southbound holdings and eligible-product analysis used to distinguish Mainland demand from general market turnover.
- `南方基金QDII产品申购限额_20260717.xlsx` - Product-level verification of QDII subscription status and limits using fund notices and PCF conventions.
- `W_10_0629_hk_etf__one_counter.xlsx` - Peer and flagship ETF universe with AUM, flow, turnover, and product attributes.
- `停牌.pptx` - Supporting analysis of premium warnings, temporary suspensions, and restricted creation channels; useful for explaining why monitoring is decision-relevant.

## What This Proves

- Ability to deliver accurate, time-sensitive recurring work.
- Understanding of the link between creations/redemptions, arbitrage capacity, premium/discount behavior, and execution liquidity.
- Ability to turn monitoring data into signals for Capital Markets and product teams rather than merely updating tables.
