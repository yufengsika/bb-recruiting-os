# Point 3 - Cross-Border Leveraged Product Market Impact

## Core Story

This point demonstrates market-structure research: reconstructing a daily leverage reset, estimating potential hedging flow, normalizing it against closing liquidity, and testing the relationship with underlying-equity behavior.

## File Map

- `CSOP_7709_Daily_Rebalance_Impact_Study_—_Instruction_Prompt_for_AI_Execution.docx` - Research question, prospectus-based formulas, data definitions, treatment/control design, and output requirements.
- `CSOP_7709_Rebalance_23Jun2026.xlsx` - Initial single-day reconstruction of the rebalance calculation.
- `CSOP_7709_Rebalance_MultiDate (2).xlsx` - Multi-date extreme-versus-control comparison using closing-hour turnover.
- `CSOP_7709_Rebalance_Study_30Days (1).xlsx` - Extended 30-day study with summary statistics and impact-ratio analysis.

## What This Proves

- Ability to translate product mechanics into a testable market question.
- Ability to combine product AUM/NAV, leverage mechanics, FX, and intraday underlying-market data.
- Good research discipline: use of a control group and explicit acknowledgement that correlation does not establish causality.

## Interview Guardrail

Describe swap-counterparty hedging as a potential transmission mechanism. Do not claim direct observation of counterparties' trades or definitive proof that product flows caused the closing move.
